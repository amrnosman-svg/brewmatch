Privacy

Last updated October 2026

BrewHarbor is a free quiz that recommends coffee machines. There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name or email.

What we collect

  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics 4), only if you accept. Pages viewed, quiz steps completed, buttons clicked, approximate location (country/region), device and browser type. Your quiz answers (drink types, effort level, budget range, region) are sent as anonymous events so we can improve the recommendations.
  • Session insights (Microsoft Clarity), only if you accept. Clarity records how visitors scroll and click, including session replays and heatmaps, so we can find confusing parts of the site. Clarity masks text you type.
  • Feedback you choose to give. If you rate your results or type a comment in the feedback box, that text is sent to Google Analytics along with your quiz answers. Please don't include personal details in it.
  • Hosting logs. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are off by default. The first time you visit, we ask whether Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may set cookies. If you say no, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, neither one is loaded. If you say yes, they set cookies to tell repeat visits apart.

Your choice is remembered in your browser for 12 months. You can change it any time with Cookie settings in the footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. We don't use cookies for advertising.

Buy links

The "Check price on Amazon" buttons send you to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases: if you buy after clicking one of those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon may set its own cookies on its site, and Amazon's privacy notice applies there. Nothing a retailer pays ever affects which machines the quiz recommends.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, run ads, or build profiles of individual visitors. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
